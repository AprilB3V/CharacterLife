# Code of Conduct

CharacterLife adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone taking part in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions
and other community spaces is expected to follow it.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people, and don't
harass anyone. This includes people writing in a language that is not their first.

Because this project builds AI characters, one point deserves spelling out: don't use the project's
spaces to share sexual content involving minors, content that sexualises real people without their
consent, or characters built to harass a real person. Such content is removed and its authors may be
banned.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
(the AprilB3V account) or use the private reporting route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer decides on the response, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant, from a private correction up to a permanent ban.
